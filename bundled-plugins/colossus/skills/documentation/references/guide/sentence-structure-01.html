Sentence structure

If you want to tell the reader to do something, try to mention the circumstance, conditions, or goal before you provide the instruction. Mentioning the circumstance first lets the reader skip the instruction if it doesn't apply. For information about how to apply this guideline to procedural instructions, see Procedures.
